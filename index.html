Forward kinematics of a 2R robot

Two revolute joints determine the position of the end effector in the plane.

The configuration is \( \mathbf{q} = [q_1,\;q_2]^T \).

\[ \mathbf{p} = f(\mathbf{q}) \]

Forward kinematics of a 2R robot

The configuration of a 2R robot is determined by two revolute joint angles.

\[ \mathbf{q} = \begin{bmatrix} q_1 \\ q_2 \end{bmatrix} \]

The end-effector position is obtained from forward kinematics:

\[ \mathbf{p}=f(\mathbf{q}) \]
