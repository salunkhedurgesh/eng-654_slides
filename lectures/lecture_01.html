How do we convert a physical robot into a reproducible mathematical model?

Opening motivation: reachable is not always executable

A pose can be reachable while a path to it is not safely executable.

One pose may admit several joint solutions.

A Cartesian line may cross a joint limit or singular region.

Planning must respect the geometry of the robot, not only the endpoint.

Configuration and configuration space

For an \(n\)-joint serial robot, one configuration is a vector

\[\mathbf{q}=[q_1,\ldots,q_n]^T.\]

The configuration space is the set of all admissible joint vectors.

Coordinate frames are part of the model

The same physical point has different numerical coordinates in different frames.

In all Three.js course visualizations, the robotics world is z-up. The root group is rotated by \(R_x(-\pi/2)\).

\[R(q)=e^{[\boldsymbol{\omega}]q}\]

Homogeneous transformations

\[ {^A T_B}=\begin{bmatrix} {^A R_B} & {^A\mathbf{p}_B}\\ \mathbf{0}^T & 1 \end{bmatrix} \]

Rotation block: orientation of frame \(B\) expressed in frame \(A\).

Translation block: position of the origin of \(B\) expressed in frame \(A\).

Compatibility rule: repeated frame indices must match during composition.
